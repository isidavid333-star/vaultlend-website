▣ VaultLend
ETH-BACKED LENDING · POLYGON

Borrow with confidence.
Keep control of your collateral.

VaultLend is a concept for ETH-backed USDT loans. Borrowers deposit collateral into a smart contract, then the company reviews loan applications.

PROTOTYPE ONLY: This website does not accept deposits, issue loans, transfer USDT, or process refunds. Do not send cryptocurrency for this demo.

How VaultLend is designed to work

01 · COLLATERAL

Deposit ETH collateral

Borrowers would connect a wallet and deposit supported collateral into a smart contract.

02 · REVIEW

Company reviews

The company reviews the application and collateral before deciding whether to fund a loan.

03 · REPAY

Repay and unlock

After repayment, the contract is intended to release eligible collateral. Refund rights must be enforced on-chain.

Security comes first

A production launch requires audited smart contracts, secure administrator authentication, verified token contracts, reliable price feeds, clear loan terms, and legal and compliance review.
